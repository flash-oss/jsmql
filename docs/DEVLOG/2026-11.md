## 2026-11-06 — fix: a method after a field read of a joined document compiles

The join road refused a method that came after a field read or an index read of the
joined value. The compiler peeled only the field reads at the end of the chain. The compiler
kept a method after such a read in the chain proper. The chain reader then found a
field read where it expects a method or `$$$.<coll>`.

```js
$ = { kyc: $$$.kycs.find({ _id: $.vanId }).nameDetails.pick(["firstName", "lastName"]) };  // refused before
```

Now the chain proper is the run of method calls directly on `$$$.<coll>`. Everything after
the first read is a value over the joined slot. A stream method after such a read, for example
`.filter(p)[0].aggregate(…)`, now gets the refusal of a stream method on a value. Before, it got
the general refusal of a join chain. See
[src/compiler/emit/join.ts](../../src/compiler/emit/join.ts) `lookupOf` and
[docs/specs/emit-pass.md](../specs/emit-pass.md) § The join road. The cases are in
[test/compiler-join.test.ts](../../test/compiler-join.test.ts).
